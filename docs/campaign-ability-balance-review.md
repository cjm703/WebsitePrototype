# Campaign Ability Pack — Balance and Consistency Review

Status: draft review for a level-12 campaign using the Eight Stages of Magic. The website import preserves the supplied mechanics, with wording normalized only enough to fit the card editor. Recommended mechanical changes below are **not** automatically applied.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| -2 | Significantly underpowered or unusable |
| -1 | Somewhat weak for its cost and unlock level |
| 0 | Campaign-balanced, including intentionally powerful abilities with meaningful costs, commitments, and counterplay |
| +1 | Above the campaign's normal power budget; playable with monitoring, clarification, or a targeted revision |
| +2 | Overwhelms meaningful counterplay even at this campaign's high power level; material revision recommended |

The ratings assume a high-powered, 5e-like d20 action economy, four combat rounds per in-world minute, and a maximum character level of 12. They do **not** use ordinary 5e character power as the target. Star Polaris is the campaign's benchmark for a deliberately powerful but balanced apex ability: its strength is paid for through crystals, limited uses, both Zodiac slots, loss of other casting/forms, an attackable heart, and level-gated scaling.

## Zodiac classification

The campaign uses three mechanically distinct Zodiac groups:

| Group | Website family | Magic? | Concentration and cost rules | Design role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Western Zodiac | Spell | Yes | Every Western spell requires concentration. Starting any other concentration spell or magical ability ends the active Western spell. Western spells use their listed source or consumed crystals. | Spell effects governed by the Eight Stages of Magic. |
| Eastern Zodiac | Ability / Form | No | Eastern Forms do not use concentration, magic, or crystals. Only one Eastern Form can be active at a time. | Utility-first transformations; combat output should remain secondary to a dedicated spell or martial skill. |
| Central Zodiac | Central Form | Magical non-spell | A Central Form occupies both Western and Eastern slots, ends the active forms/spells in those slots, and prevents their use while it persists. | Rare apex form with an explicit vulnerability and long-rest limit. |

Because Eastern Forms are non-magical, they are not spells and are not placed in Magic lists. Unless the campaign defines an exception, they cannot be counterspelled or dispelled and do not end in an antimagic area. That makes unlimited combat riders on an Eastern Form more—not less—valuable, so their balance recommendations still apply.

## Magic-stage rulings

Character unlock level and magic stage are stored separately. A spell learned at character Level 1 can still be Stage 6 magic if its magical operation establishes a domain.

| Spell | Recommended stage | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Fae Circle of the Wild Hold | 6 — Dominion | It creates an eight-hour sanctuary whose glamour continuously governs perception of an area. |
| Twilight's Decree | 4 — Branching | It uses the specialized Dusk/Twilight branch to suppress several related forms of concealment within a zone. |
| Twilight Binding | 2 — Precision | It selectively shapes Dusk into a detailed restraint against one target. |
| Horizon's Lament | 2 — Precision | It precisely suspends one ally at the edge of defeat when the triggering event occurs. |

None of the submitted spells qualify as Stage 7 or 8. They do not temporarily rewrite a law for a city-scale subject or permanently alter the setting's laws.

### Central-form shared rule

Add this rule verbatim to both Central forms:

> A Central Form occupies both your Western and Eastern Zodiac form slots. Entering it immediately ends every other Zodiac form and any spell that requires your active form. While it persists, you cannot enter another form or cast a spell. You cannot benefit from two instances of the same Central Form.

This makes the user's two-slot requirement enforceable at the table even though the website currently tracks it as card text rather than a resource slot.

## Consistency rules for the website

1. **Spell stage is not character level.** Western spell cards use `Level` and Magic tier for Stage 1–8. Character acquisition belongs in Lotus's Level categories.
2. **Eastern Forms are non-magical abilities.** They use an Eastern form slot, never appear in a Magic list, require no crystals, and do not compete for concentration.
3. **Western spells always require concentration.** Beginning any other concentration magic ends the active Western Zodiac spell.
4. **Abilities and skills use character unlock level.** Their `Level` value is the acquisition level because they are not placed in Magic tiers.
5. **Rest limits use one format.** Use `1 / Short Rest`, `2 / Long Rest`, or `PB / Long Rest`.
6. **Every reaction states a trigger and timing.** “After the roll but before damage” is materially different from “when targeted.”
7. **Every save states when it repeats.** Persistent restraint or other severe control should normally allow a save at the end of each affected turn.
8. **Four rounds is the mechanical duration.** The cards retain “1 minute (4 rounds)” for setting flavor, but automation should use four rounds.
9. **Temporary HP does not stack.** A creature keeps the higher amount unless a card explicitly creates a separate pool.
10. **Direct assignment is avoided by the importer.** Level cards come from player Level categories, spells come from Magic lists, and the two Central forms come only from unlocked matching nodes.
11. **Imported spells start unlearned.** They are available in Lotus's Magic lists but do not appear as learned player cards until selected on the player side.
12. **Stable IDs make import idempotent.** Repeated imports skip existing cards rather than duplicating or overwriting DM edits.
13. **Bleed needs one shared rule.** Define damage per stack, when it triggers, how it is removed, whether a new stack refreshes its duration, and the maximum number of stacks.
14. **Eastern Forms remain non-magical.** References to a “magical” staff, needle, or damage type affect that attack's interaction with resistance; they do not make the form a spell, add a magic cost, require concentration, or make the form dispellable.
15. **SIN is a separate fourth card family.** SINs awaken through Emotion and use their own activation and falsification rules. They are neither spells, skills, nor abilities; do not infer a magic stage, source cost, concentration requirement, or character level from a SIN card. The Stage of Pride is an unassigned catalog card until the DM grants it to a character.

## Recommended approval order

Resolve these before play because they still overwhelm counterplay at the raised baseline: Flow of Steel and Stalwart Defiance.

The `+1` cards are above baseline but can be playtested after their targeted revisions. Rating-`0` cards are campaign-balanced; any listed clarification is about consistent adjudication rather than a required nerf.
