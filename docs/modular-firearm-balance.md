# Modular Firearm Balance Sheet

This sheet is the baseline for starter Workshop firearms. A frame defines the weapon's damage, range, capacity, reload, handedness, and core trait. Ammunition and optional modules modify that profile; they do not replace it unless their effect explicitly says so.

## Starter Frame Baselines

Required build cost includes the 500-credit construction price, 80-credit standard crystal ammunition system, and 220-credit standard modular barrel.

| Frame | Required build | Hands | Damage | Average | Range | Capacity | Reload | Role |

| Pistol | 1,150 | One | 2d6 piercing | 7 | 50/150 ft | 10 | Bonus action | Flexible sidearm with reliable capacity |
| Revolver | 1,200 | One | 2d8 piercing | 9 | 60/180 ft | 6 | Bonus action | Stronger individual hits with fewer shots |
| Shotgun | 1,450 | Two | 3d6 at 0-15 ft, 2d6 at 16-30 ft, 1d6 at 31-90 ft | 10.5/7/3.5 | 30/90 ft | 2 | Action | Highest close-range pressure; steep distance and reload costs |

## Part Influence

| Part or slot | Price | Influence |

| Standard Crystal Ammunition System | 80 | Supplies the standard magic-crystal reload; adds no second damage die |
| Standard Modular Barrel | 220 | Adds 30 ft to both normal and maximum range |

## Stacking Rules

1. Install exactly one frame, one ammunition system, and one barrel before construction can begin.
2. Use the frame's damage once. Standard ammunition does not add another base damage roll.
3. Add the barrel's 30 ft to both range values, then apply the suppressor's 10 ft penalty to normal range only.
4. A sight and a stock can provide at most +2 Accuracy together; the stock's +1 only applies while stationary and braced.
5. Special ammunition changes damage type or adds its written rider to the frame profile. It does not silently replace capacity or reload rules.
6. The automatic frame's controlled burst consumes 3 rounds and makes two attacks with disadvantage. It does not triple damage automatically.

## Balance Notes

- Required builds cost more than a fixed production weapon because the finished item can be rebuilt and reconfigured.
- Revolver and rifle damage are intentionally high, but limited by capacity, reload economy, or two-handed use.
- The shotgun leads at very close range but falls below the sidearms outside 15 ft.
- Abyss Fabricator magazines are campaign-tier effects. Incendiary, Toxic, and Echo ammunition can substantially exceed this baseline and should be treated as limited-use rewards rather than ordinary purchased ammunition.
- Compare future parts against a 1-point Accuracy bonus, 30 ft of range, or one modest rules benefit. A part granting two of those should carry a drawback or a noticeably higher price.
